# Product images

The product photos in this folder come from [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org),
contributed by its community. They are licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
Product names, barcodes and pack sizes come from the Open Food Facts database
([ODbL](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/)).

Each file is named `<barcode>.jpg`. Its source page is
`https://world.openfoodfacts.org/product/<barcode>` (also recorded per product in
`apps/api/src/scripts/data/demo-catalog.json`).

Prices in the demo catalog are illustrative estimates, not from Open Food Facts.

The four `89000000000*.svg` files are original illustrations for the fictional seed brands
(Brand X, Brand Y, Brand M) and are not from Open Food Facts.
